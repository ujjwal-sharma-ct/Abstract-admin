Releases, proposals, Agency-sourced bookings and absence confirmations carry across the three portals when the design folder is served from one address.

The demo path

  1. Open VAC-1042: TechForce Recruitment has it (wave 1). Choose Release to agencies, tick Nexus Talent (Summit Staffing Solutions is not active), Release to 1: wave 2 appears in the Release History (TV-1, TV-3).
  2. Open VAC-1043: every position is filled, so release is not available (TV-2).
  3. On VAC-1041 (Released to Agencies, two waves), review the proposals: start the review of PRP-1006, accept it on position 1, then accept PRP-1009 and PRP-1005. The last acceptance closes PRP-1010 as Rejected (Position Filled) (TV-4).
  4. See the Agency-sourced bookings in Bookings, filter by agency, and the By agency counts on the dashboard (TV-5, TV-6).
  5. Open Kofi Mensah’s week of 5 Oct: Wednesday reads Awaiting agency (R5-D6); ask for confirmation of Thursday. His week of 28 Sep shows the agency’s reason on a Justified day, the no-show whose pay Abstract overrode, and is invoiced (TV-8).
  6. In the agency portal, TechForce gives the reason for Wednesday; reload the week here and it reads Justified.

IDs refer to the slice 5 design brief, Part A, and to the TSD’s 5.6 capability table (TV-n). Where the brief and the TSD differ, the pages follow the TSD.
