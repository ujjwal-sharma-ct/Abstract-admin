This is where you set up who can use Abstract VMS and how it behaves: people, roles and permissions for Abstract and every client and agency, the system's settings, and the messages it sends. Pick a place to start.

People and access

Users Invite people, set their roles, deactivate or reactivate accounts. Roles Create, rename and deactivate the roles your users hold. Permission matrix Choose what each role can see and do. Organisations Every client and agency — administer their users and roles.

Configuration

System configuration Effective-dated settings, shift patterns and bank holidays. Notification templates The wording of the emails and in-app messages this system sends.

Oversight and privacy

Audit log Every change, who made it and when, with before and after values. Data subject requests Compile, export or erase a person's personal data. My account Your name, your sign-in email and your password.
