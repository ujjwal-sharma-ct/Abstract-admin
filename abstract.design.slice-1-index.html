Slice 1 mode hides every navigation link and control that belongs to a later slice, and sends "Dashboard" to the slice 1 placeholder. It is off.

Every slice 1 screen has a Prototype menu (bottom left) to switch its states — loading, empty, error, validation, permission denied, large data — and to trigger the session-timeout warning.

IDs refer to the slice 1 design brief: A = no design before, B = sketched, C = correction to an existing export. P1 = needed for the Abstract demo.
