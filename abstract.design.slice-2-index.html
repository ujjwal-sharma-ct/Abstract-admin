Slice 2 mode shows the slice 1 and slice 2 screens together as the product at slice 2's close: every navigation link and control that belongs to slice 3 or later is hidden. Navigation is —. · ·

Every screen has a Prototype menu (bottom) to switch its states — loading, empty, error, validation, permission denied, large data, and the page's own — and ?state=<name> opens one directly. The wizards frame the other pages with ?embedded=1.

IDs refer to the slice 2 design brief: A = no design before, B = sketched, C = correction to an existing export. P1 = the demo path.
