Approve an agency for a client

Today or later. From that date the client's vacancies can be released to this agency. A re-approval after a revocation is a new dated record.

Revoke approval

Today or later, and not before the approval date. From that date no new vacancy of this client is released to this agency.

Existing releases, proposals and bookings are untouched. Revoking only stops future releases. This is audited.
