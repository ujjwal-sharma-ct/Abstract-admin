The demo path

  1. As the client (client portal), open this week’s grid. Select the rows, choose Fill week, review the preview, then save and confirm.
  2. Submit Marek Nowak’s week (Leeds Hub: approved at submission) and Nadia Hussain’s (Birmingham DC: waits for Abstract). Mark the rest Not Booked where a day is still blank.
  3. See Tasha Williams’s week held by two days waiting for manual pricing, and the critical notification in both portals.
  4. As Abstract, set a price for both days from Pending pricing. The week is no longer held and can be submitted.
  5. Approve Nadia’s week from the oversight list. Open its pay statement in Payroll.
  6. Reopen the week with a reason and approve it again: version 1 reads superseded, version 2 is current.

IDs refer to the slice 4 design brief: A = no design before, C = correction to an existing export. P1 = the demo path. Where the brief and the rulings of 8 Nov 2026 differ, the pages follow the rulings.
